Privacy Policy for Circle

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Welcome to Circle ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our dating application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with a safe and enjoyable dating experience. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, age, gender, photos, bio, and interests.
  • Contact Information: Email address and phone number for authentication.
  • Location Data: With your explicit consent, we process precise geolocation to show you potential matches in your area.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, including matches, likes, and messages.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, IP address, and unique identifiers for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the Circle service.
  • To facilitate matching with other users based on your preferences.
  • To verify user authenticity and prevent fraudulent activities or harassment.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To improve app features, security, and user experience.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following scenarios:

  • With Other Users: Information you choose to put on your profile is visible to other users.
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors that assist us with cloud hosting, security, and analytics.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process to protect our rights or the safety of our users.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption, to protect your data. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account data at any time via the app settings. You can also withdraw your consent for location tracking in your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Circle is intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of any significant changes via the app or email.
