Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Safety Standards

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Circle is a social discovery and dating application designed for adults. The safety of our users is a priority, and we have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Important: Circle is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. Individuals under the age of 18 are not permitted to create or use an account on Circle.

Age Requirement

Circle is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. Individuals under the age of 18 are not permitted to create or use an account on Circle.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

Circle strictly prohibits the following content and behavior:

  • Any sexual content involving children or minors.
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish sexual relationships with minors.
  • Sharing, requesting, producing, distributing, or promoting child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual solicitation involving minors.
  • Using Circle to facilitate or promote the exploitation or trafficking of children.
  • Any other behavior that facilitates child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Reporting

Users can report accounts, messages, posts, stories, or other content that they believe violates our safety standards.

Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated as a high-priority safety matter.

Users should provide as much relevant information as possible when submitting a report.

Enforcement

Circle may take immediate action against accounts involved in suspected CSAE violations, including:

  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Restricting or suspending accounts.
  • Permanently terminating accounts.
  • Preserving relevant information when appropriate and permitted by law.
  • Reporting suspected illegal activity to appropriate authorities or relevant organizations when required by applicable law.

User Safety

Users should never attempt to arrange sexual contact with minors or use Circle to facilitate inappropriate interactions with anyone under 18.

If you believe a child may be in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services or law enforcement authorities in your country.
